About this data. Compiled from publicly listed sources — operator websites, booking platforms and official sports bodies. Not an official government dataset. Fields such as village, panchayat, usage_fee and pool_depth are incomplete where operators don't publish them, and read "Not stated" rather than being guessed. Verify contact details before relying on them.
